Working with us

What do I need to send for a quote?

Drawings or 3D files if you have them (STEP, IGES, STL, DWG or PDF). If not, a sketch, photos of an existing part, or a description of what the product must do. Add the quantity and the date you need it.

Can you sign an NDA before I share my design?

Yes. Tick "NDA needed" on the form, or ask by email, and we will send our mutual NDA or sign yours.

I only have an idea. Can you still help?

Yes. That is what our product development service is for. We start with a short brief and propose a design and material.

Who owns the design and the molds?

It is agreed in each quotation, before any work starts, so you know up front who owns the design files and the molds.

Processes and materials

Which process is right for my part?

It depends on size, shape, quantity, surface finish and cost. As a guide: prepreg for light, stiff structures; vacuum infusion for larger parts; light RTM for repeat parts with two good surfaces; hand layup for large or low-volume parts. We recommend one with every quote.

Carbon fiber or glass fiber?

Carbon fiber is stiffer and lighter but costs more. Glass fiber is tougher for the money and fine for most covers, panels and blades. We help you choose based on what the part must do.

Do you use an autoclave?

No. We make parts by oven-cured prepreg, vacuum infusion, light RTM, wet and hand layup. For most parts these give the quality needed at lower cost. If your part needs autoclave cure, we will tell you at the review stage.

Do you supply materials on their own?

Yes. Our design engineers advise on the right material for your part, then we supply it to order with datasheet, safety data sheet and certificate of conformity.

Quantities, time and delivery

What is your minimum order?

No fixed minimum. We make single prototypes and production batches.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the part and the quantity. Every quotation states the lead time for molds, first parts and production.

Do you ship outside Pakistan?

Yes. We ship by air and sea to the Gulf, the Middle East and worldwide, with export documents. You choose ex works (EXW) or delivered to your door (DAP); duties and taxes at destination are paid by the buyer.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer or Payoneer against our invoice, in US dollars (or Pakistani rupees for buyers in Pakistan). For manufacturing and CNC orders we ask for 50% in advance and 50% before shipping. Card payment will be added later.

Quality

How do you check the parts?

Molds and parts are checked against your CAD by 3D scanning, and the first article is inspected before production. Each material batch comes with its certificate of conformity.

Not at present.
