From idea to part

How a part comes to life

01

Design & analysis

Your drawing or idea becomes a laminate design, checked with FEA before any material is cut.

02

Tooling

Master patterns are CNC machined, then molds are built and finished for a clean part surface.

03

Layup & infusion

Carbon or glass fabric is laid in the mold and wetted by hand layup or vacuum infusion, depending on the part.

04

Trim & finish

Cured parts are demolded, trimmed to the line and finished to your drawing.

05

Inspect & deliver

Each part is checked against the drawing before it is packed and shipped.
