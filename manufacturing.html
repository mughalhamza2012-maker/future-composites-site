Patterns and molds, made in-house

  • Master patterns and molds machined on our own 3-axis CNC, in tooling board and high-density MDF
  • Molds designed for clean release and paint-ready surfaces
  • Molds checked by 3D scanning against your CAD model

Checked before it ships
