Three things we machine

Most composite parts start on a CNC. So do metal molds and the brackets, housings and fittings that go around them. One team handles all three, so your pattern, your mold and your parts fit together.

1. Patterns and master models

Master patterns and plugs cut from tooling board, high-density MDF and foam on our own 3-axis CNC. We seal and finish the surface, then check it against your CAD by 3D scanning. The pattern is used to take a composite mold, or the board is machined as a direct mold for short runs.

Made in: our Islamabad workshop.

2. Molds and tools in metal

Aluminum and steel molds for parts that need a long tool life, heat or pressure: prepreg and press molding, RTM, injection mold inserts, vacuum-forming and casting tools. Low-expansion Invar tools on request. Hardened inserts, carbide and ceramic inserts, EDM detail and optical-grade surfaces through our precision partners.

Made in: our manufacturing network, designed and checked by our engineers.

3. Parts manufacturing

Metal and plastic parts made to your drawing: brackets, housings, fittings, inserts, jigs and fixtures. One prototype or a small batch on our 3-axis CNC. Production runs on 3-, 4- and 5-axis mills, mill-turn and Swiss-type lathes, with finishing and inspection reports.

Made in: Islamabad for prototypes, our network for volume.

From pattern to mold to part

  1. Pattern. We machine the shape of your part in tooling board or MDF and finish it to a mold-ready surface.
  2. Mold. We take a composite mold from the pattern, or machine the mold directly in aluminum or steel when the part needs heat, pressure or thousands of cycles.
  3. Part. We make the composite part in the mold, and machine the metal and plastic parts that fit to it.
  4. Check. Each step is measured against your CAD, so the part you get matches the drawing you sent.

Need only one step? We also machine patterns, molds or parts on their own. More on CNC master patterns and carbon fiber molds.
