How a custom part is made

  1. Review. You send a drawing, 3D file or sketch. An engineer asks about loads, finish, quantity and deadline.
  2. Design. We choose the fiber (1K to 12K, plain or twill, UD), the resin and the ply layup, and run FEA when the part carries load.
  3. Pattern and mold. We machine the pattern on our 3-axis CNC and build the mold.
  4. Parts. We make the parts by wet layup with vacuum bag, oven-cured prepreg, vacuum infusion or bladder molding, then trim, drill and bond inserts.
  5. Check. Parts are measured against your CAD. First article inspection comes before any batch.

Processes and what they suit

We do not use an autoclave. For most industrial, drone and sports parts, oven-cured prepreg or infusion gives the stiffness needed at lower cost.

Questions about custom parts

Is there a minimum order?

No. We make single prototypes. The mold is the main cost, so the price per part falls as the quantity grows.

Can you design the part for me?

Yes. Send the idea, the size and what it must do. We design it, show you the CAD and quote the parts.

Will my drawings stay confidential?

Yes. We sign an NDA on request, and we never show client parts or names without permission.

How long does it take?

It depends on size and quantity. We give a schedule with the quotation, covering the mold and the first parts.
